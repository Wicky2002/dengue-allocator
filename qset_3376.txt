## Question set 1 — for Public

**1. Baseline (before showing them anything):**
> "Do you think dengue is something to worry about in [their district] this week? And roughly how many cases do you think there are nearby?"

Write down: their answer, how long they took (rough seconds/minutes), and *"On a scale of 1–5, how sure are you of that guess?"*

**2. Hand them the tool** (district lookup, already open): *"Find out if you should actually be worried, and see how many cases are forecast."* Don't help — just time it and watch quietly.

**3. After they find it:**
> "So — should you be worried? How many cases?"

Write down their new answer + time taken, then *"Now how sure are you, 1–5?"*

**4. Five 1–5 ratings:** Easy to use? / Trusted the numbers? / Would use weekly? / Faster than usual? / Confusing at any point? *(reversed — 5 = very confusing)*

**5. Open question:** "What would you change about it?"

**6. Note yourself:** completed unaided Y/N, anything they got stuck on.

**Optional, only if it happens naturally:** if they scroll down and notice the "View a past week" slider on their own, jot their reaction (that's column AA now) — don't specifically point them to it.

Send me name/relation + district, both rounds' answers/times/confidence, the 5 ratings, unaided Y/N, what they'd change, and any friction — same format as before.
